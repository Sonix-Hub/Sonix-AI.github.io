🔐 Where does my data actually live?›
👤 Guest mode
Everything — chats, sessions, settings — stays only in this browser's local storage. Nothing syncs anywhere. Closing the tab or clearing site data loses it for good.
☁️ Signed-in account
Your conversations and settings sync to our Cloudflare database, so you can pick up where you left off on another device. Custom model API keys are the one exception — those always stay local to the browser that added them, never synced.

Worth knowing:
• "Export Chat" gives you a personal backup file either way — SONIX keeps no copy of it once it leaves your device.
• Deleting your account clears everything tied to it, both locally and on our server.
